## Module: second opinion from another model

Consult a different model family only (a) before shipping a change where money or a statistical verdict depends on assumptions (look-ahead, survivorship, leakage, cost claims), or (b) when a decision-relevant contradiction survives one targeted local check. Send a brief (state, evidence, your own thesis, the question, the critic findings marked "known, do not repeat"), not an open repository. At most one consultation per work package, inside a fixed quota. The answer is data: the lead decides on every point and records acceptance or rejection.
