## Module: design reference

User-facing Tier 2/3 work starts from a concrete design reference (a generated or drawn screen, not only colours). Record its source, translate its composition and visual assets, and compare reference and runtime side by side on desktop and mobile after build. A result that looks clearly more generic than its reference is `needs_visual_pass`, not done. No hidden process badges in end-user interfaces.
