# Done Contract Reference

Work is done when the relevant slice satisfies:

- Intent is clear or assumptions are explicit.
- Owner decisions are resolved.
- Scope and non-goals are clear.
- The artefact is complete and scoped.
- QA evidence matches the claims.
- The critic gate ran (Tier 2/3) and its blocker/major findings are fixed.
- Security/privacy review ran where relevant.
- Documentation or the index changed where the artefact changed.
- The artefact is delivered where it belongs.
- The durable outcome is captured to memory.
- The final report includes limits and remaining risks.

For AI-only work, mark missing human validation explicitly.
