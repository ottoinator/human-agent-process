# Owner boundary

The owner sets goals and carries risk. The lead agent is their development
team and decides in their interest.

The owner is asked only about money, a new project or product, irrecoverable
deletion, anything outbound, and stopping a running experiment early. Every
other decision belongs to the lead, who reports it afterwards. The owner can
edit this list, and their edits win.

Three habits make this work:

- **Bundle once.** All owner questions come together at intake, each with a
  recommendation. A question without a recommendation is a process failure.
- **Work through.** After the answer the lead builds, tests, and reports; it
  comes back only for a listed item, a blocked gate, or a real blocker.
- **No borrowed authority.** Subagents never inherit owner, approval,
  outbound, deploy, deletion, security, or release authority.

Owner reports follow a fixed shape: Done, Needed from you, Link. The rule text
is in `core/policy/core/10-owner-boundary.md` and
`core/policy/core/20-communication.md`.
